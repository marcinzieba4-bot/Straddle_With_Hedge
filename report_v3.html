Straddle Hedge Tearsheet

Monthly ATM short straddle, premium repriced off each asset's implied-vol index, hedged by one unit of future/underlying whose side follows a daily Renko signal with per-asset flip hysteresis. Realistic conditions throughout: 90%/110% bid-ask marks, per-fill fees, next-open hedge fills. Returns are % of each month's spot, summed arithmetically.
